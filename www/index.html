about us

Hi, I’m Carrie Mah.

I spent 15 years in tech before severe burnout and concussions forced me off screens. Suddenly, I felt lost without my job title. It started with asking myself: When was the last time I actually had fun?

I thought revisiting a childhood passion of drawing could help me unwind. But staring at a blank page felt intimidating. So, I started repurposing existing stationery into junk journal spreads and collage art.

This lit a 🔥 fire in me. After years of struggling with burnout, I was finally fuelled with creative energy again. I realized that just resting from work wasn't enough—I needed to reinvigorate my soul using my hands.

I built the Always Be Creative Club to share this energy. Since March 2026, we've guided over 70 adults, secured local partnerships, and built a circular ecosystem where we upcycle vintage materials and support local businesses.

We’re here to help you overcome perfectionism, share the joy of making art, and remind you that you can Always Be Creative ✨

Warmly,

Carrie, Founder
